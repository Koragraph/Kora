Fixes from the first beta pass.

- Settings: Back always takes you back to where you were. Before, you could get stuck in Settings.
- Unknown or missing pages show "Nothing lives at this address" with a way back, instead of a blank screen.
- When Kora can't reach its background service, it says what to do and links Diagnostics.
- Projects: "Project settings" in every project's menu; the "Folder missing" note links to it.
- Keyboard: focus returns to the right place after renaming a thread or picking workers, and thread menus close with Escape.
- Smaller fixes: the archived count, wording, and a centred send button.
