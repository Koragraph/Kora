# Code of Conduct

Kora's community follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, give and take criticism about the work and never the person,
and remember that people contribute in their own time. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and
sharing anyone's private information are not acceptable in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any channel run for the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/Koragraph/Kora/security/advisories/new) (choose "Report a
vulnerability" and say it is a conduct report), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants, following the enforcement guidelines
of the Contributor Covenant.
