Kora's source code is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The license covers the code, not the name or the
logo.

"Kora", the Kora sprite mark and the app icon identify the official app and its releases. You may:

- say that your project uses, works with or is based on Kora;
- show the name in plain text when referring to the project.

Please do not:

- distribute a modified build under the name Kora or with the Kora icon;
- use the name or mark in a way that suggests your product is official or endorsed.

If you ship a fork, give it its own name and icon (the icons are generated by `scripts/brand/make-icons.py`, so
changing the sprite map there produces a full new set). Questions: open a discussion.
